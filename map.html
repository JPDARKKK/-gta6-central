GTA 6 Map: Vice City and Leonida Explained

Grand Theft Auto VI returns to a fictional version of Florida, including Vice City and the wider state of Leonida.

Last editorial review: November 20, 2026

We distinguish official announcements from speculation. Check the linked official sources for the latest details.

What is Vice City?

Vice City is a fictional urban setting inspired by Miami and is a recognizable location in the Grand Theft Auto series. Official promotional material introduces a modern version of the area.

What is Leonida?

Leonida is the fictional state surrounding Vice City. Official previews present a broader regional setting beyond the city itself. Specific fan-made map boundaries should not be mistaken for an official interactive map.

How to interpret map leaks

Fan reconstructions can be creative and informative, but they are not the same as a developer-published map. Compare any claimed location with the official trailer or published materials before calling it confirmed.
