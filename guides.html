GTA 6 Beginner Guide: Get Ready to Play

A practical checklist for players who want to prepare without falling for fake downloads or misleading claims.

Last editorial review: November 20, 2026

We distinguish official announcements from speculation. Check the linked official sources for the latest details.

Use official stores

For game availability and supported platforms, check Rockstar and the storefront on your console. Avoid websites offering unofficial early-access installers, activation keys or suspicious downloads.

Check your setup

Make sure your console has enough free storage, install system updates and review accessibility settings such as subtitles, controls and display preferences. Exact technical requirements should be verified from official listings.

Avoid spoilers and misinformation

Mute spoiler-heavy keywords on social media if you want to discover the story yourself. Treat alleged hidden locations, secret missions and online-mode claims as unverified until supported by reliable evidence.

Play at your own pace

Explore the world, read in-game tutorials and experiment with settings. Our guides focus on clear explanations rather than unsupported promises about shortcuts.
