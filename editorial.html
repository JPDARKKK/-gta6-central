Our Editorial Standards

Editorial Policy

How GTA 6 Central researches, labels, updates and corrects its gaming coverage.

1. Accuracy first

We aim to publish clear, useful information and avoid presenting rumors or speculation as established facts. When reporting an announcement, we prefer official Rockstar Games statements and primary materials.

2. Source transparency

We identify relevant primary sources and provide links where possible. We distinguish official announcements, direct observations, analysis and third-party reports. A source link does not imply endorsement.

3. Rumors and uncertainty

Unverified claims are labeled clearly, and we avoid inventing release dates, gameplay features, pricing, technical specifications or other details. We update coverage when reliable new information becomes available.

4. Originality and attribution

We aim to write original explanations and summaries rather than copy articles from other sites. Images and media are used only when we have an appropriate basis to do so, with credit where applicable.

5. Corrections and updates

If you notice an error, email parrajuanpablo22@gmail.com with the article URL, the issue and supporting evidence. We review credible correction requests and update material inaccuracies. Significant corrections may be identified in the relevant article.

6. Advertising and independence

Editorial decisions are separate from advertising. If advertisements, sponsorships or affiliate links are introduced, we will disclose commercial relationships where appropriate. We do not claim to be affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

7. Contact

Visit our Contact page for editorial questions, privacy inquiries and corrections.
