Trailer Guide

GTA 6 Trailers: What to Watch For

Official trailers are the best place to start when learning about the game’s tone, setting and characters.

Last editorial review: November 20, 2026

We distinguish official announcements from speculation. Check the linked official sources for the latest details.

Where to watch

Use the official Rockstar Games website or its verified video channels. These sources help avoid fan edits incorrectly presented as new trailers.

Look for setting and character details

Notice the urban and coastal environments, everyday activity and the way characters are introduced. A short trailer scene does not necessarily confirm a playable feature.

Separate observations from predictions

It is fair to say a trailer shows a specific scene; it is not fair to conclude that every visible vehicle, building or action is necessarily interactive. We clearly separate observation from speculation.
