About GTA 6 Central

Independent coverage, practical guides and clear sourcing for the world of Grand Theft Auto VI.

Our purpose

GTA 6 Central is an independent English-language fan publication focused on Grand Theft Auto VI. We create accessible explainers about announced characters, locations, trailers, platforms and related developments.

How we work

We aim to distinguish confirmed announcements from interpretation, commentary and unverified reports. Where relevant, we link to primary sources, including Rockstar Games. Our articles are written for readers rather than search engines alone.
