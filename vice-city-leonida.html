Vice City and Leonida: GTA 6 Setting Explained

Understand the distinction between Vice City and the wider state of Leonida, and how to assess map claims.

Editorial review: November 20, 2026 · Facts are checked against publisher sources where available.

This is an independent fan guide. We distinguish official information from analysis and do not publish unverified leaks as facts.

Vice City is not the whole setting

Vice City is the fictional city associated with Grand Theft Auto VI, while Leonida refers to the broader fictional state. When a post calls a location “the entire map,” check whether it is discussing a neighborhood, the city or the state.

What official trailers can establish

Official footage can confirm that an environment, landmark or visual theme appears in promotional material. It does not necessarily reveal whether every visible place is accessible, where its boundaries lie or how the finished game organizes travel.

How to read community maps

Fan-made maps are useful for discussing possibilities, but their outlines and labels can be interpretations. Look for a clear legend distinguishing confirmed locations from guesses. Prefer a map with dated source links and an explanation of how locations were identified.

Why the setting matters

The contrast between urban streets, coastal spaces and other environments creates opportunities for different kinds of storytelling. Focus on what the released official material actually shows rather than assuming features from previous games will return.

Where to check for updates

Use Rockstar’s official GTA VI page and trailers as primary references. If we update this guide after a new announcement, we will identify the source and review date rather than quietly changing old claims.

Primary sources

Check Rockstar Games — Grand Theft Auto VI and Rockstar Newswire for current official statements.
