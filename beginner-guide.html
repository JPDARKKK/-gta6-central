GTA 6 Beginner’s Guide: A Practical Starting Checklist

A practical GTA 6 preparation checklist covering official information, accessibility, storage, safe purchasing and avoiding misinformation.

Editorial review: November 20, 2026 · Facts are checked against publisher sources where available.

This is an independent fan guide. We distinguish official information from analysis and do not publish unverified leaks as facts.

Start with the official source

Before relying on any guide, open Rockstar Games’ official Grand Theft Auto VI pages and the store listing for your platform. Check publication dates: an older article may describe a different development stage. Save official sources so you can revisit them when details change.

Prepare your device thoughtfully

Review the platform’s current system updates, available storage and network settings. Do not assume a particular download size unless it is shown by the store for your device. If you share a console, check which account will own a digital purchase and whether other users can access it.

Set up your preferred experience

Look through the game and system accessibility options when available. Subtitles, contrast, audio balance and controller remapping can make a long session easier to navigate. Take time to adjust the settings rather than accepting defaults.

Avoid fake downloads and promises

Unverified “PC installers,” early-access invitations and account-verification links can be misleading. Do not enter passwords on sites reached through unsolicited messages. A legitimate game listing should be traceable to the publisher or a recognized storefront.

Make a flexible first-session plan

Explore at your own pace. Use the in-game tutorial and map, learn how saving works, and revisit settings after your first session. A useful beginner guide should help you understand systems rather than spoil the story.

Primary sources

Check Rockstar Games — Grand Theft Auto VI and Rockstar Newswire for current official statements.
